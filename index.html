Just the main course, no bars

The bars compare each course against your biggest one, so they earn their keep when you study several languages. With a single course, or with one course that dwarfs the rest, turn them off:

{{< duolingo courses="1" bars="false" >}}

Overriding the username

The user option works inline too, so a page can point at a different account than the one set in the YAML:

{{< duolingo user="another-username" >}}

Every card on this page reads a single profile: the author’s own. Point the extension at accounts you have reason to publish, since the card reproduces a person’s name, avatar and activity on your site.
